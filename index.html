Intellectual Property Rights

Infringement Policy


1 Market Philippines and its affiliates (collectively, the ”we”, “us”, or “our”) are committed to ensuring that the products on its marketplaces do not infringe upon intellectual property rights (“IPR”), and we take a serious stance when dealing with claims of IPR infringement on the e-commerce platforms that we operate, including our mobile applications, and any other internet domain properties owned, controlled, or operated by the Lazada Group (collectively \"Platform\").


The users of our platform (“Users”) are strictly prohibited from engaging in IPR infringement conduct on the platform: Users who upload content, list products or services, or use the platform in any manner that infringes IPR shall be subject to the measures detailed in our platform policies, including but not limited to the issuance of non-compliance points, the suspension of user accounts, permanent termination from the platform, and/or any action deemed necessary by us, including but not limited to the initiation of civil or criminal proceedings by 1 Market Philippines independently or in conjunction with rights holders.


Listing products or services to which Users apply a sign or signs identical to a registered trademark to a product or service to establish the origin of such product or service, and the application of such sign has been conducted without the express or implied consent of the proprietor of the registered trademark, in relation to the goods or services for which that trademark is registered, or to such goods or services that are closely related to the goods or services registered;


Listing products or services to which Users apply a sign or signs similar to a registered trademark to a product or service to establish the origin of such product or service, without the express or implied consent of the proprietor of the registered trademark, in relation to the product or services for which that trademark is registered or to such goods or services that are closely related to the goods or services registered, and there exists a likelihood of confusion on the part of the public with regards to the origin of the product or services; or


Using copyrighted subject-matter (e.g., photographs, drawings, videos) in the description of the products or services, without the express or implied consent of the owner of the copyright; or


Using a sign or signs in connection with the description of products or services, and such signs are identical or very similar to a registered trademark, and the application of such sign has been conducted without the express or implied consent of the proprietor of the registered trademark, in relation to the goods or services for which that trademark is registered, and there exists a likelihood of confusion on the part of the public with regards to the origin, endorsement, or association of the product or services and the registered trademark.


Listing or products or services that infringe upon any other type of intellectual property right recognized under the laws of the country where the notice of infringement is filed, including, but not limited to, the patents, registered designs, or any type of IPR recognized under law or by the final court order of an apex court.


We respect the rights of brand owners, manufacturers, trading companies, and/or distributors to enter into agreements in relation to how their products should be distributed (such as exclusive distribution agreements). However, violations of such agreements do not constitute IPR infringement. As the enforcement of agreements relating to the distribution of products is a matter between the parties to such agreements, we do not assist with this type of enforcement activity.


1 Market Philippines does not enforce notices of infringement seeking to remove or alter true statements indicating compatibility with trademarked products. If a product detail page clearly and truthfully represents that the product being sold on that page is compatible with a trademarked product, 1 Market Philippines will not process notices directed against the use of the trademarked sign.


Note: TO SUBMIT A NOTICE OF IPR INFRINGEMENT, YOU MUST EITHER OWN OR HOLD THE EXCLUSIVE LICENSE TO THE IPR THAT IS THE SUBJECT OF THE INFRINGEMENT NOTICE OR BE AN AGENT WITH WRITTEN AND SIGNED AUTHORIZATION FROM THE RIGHTS OWNER OR EXCLUSIVE LICENSEE TO SUBMIT NOTICES ON ITS BEHALF.


Users may file appeals where the content, or product or service listing(s), which Users have uploaded, have been removed from the Platform as a result of notices filed by rights holders. Users should refer to the relevant section of 1 Market Philippines in Seller Center for more information regarding the appeal process. Where appeals by users are successful, 1 Market Philippines will reinstate the content or product or service listing(s), which have been removed.
